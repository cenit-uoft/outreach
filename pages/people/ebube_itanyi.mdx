Ebubechukwu (Anthony) Itanyi is an undergraduate student in the CENIT Research Group. 
He is pursuing his Bachelor of Applied Science (BASc) degree in Mechanical Engineering 
at the University of Toronto (UofT). He is also a recipient of the U of T Engineering International Scholar Award. 
His research is on the analysis of the internet through the sociotechnical lens. He has presented this research at 
the UofT Undergraduate Engineering Research Day (UNERD) Conference and was awarded Runner-up for Best Poster in Transdisciplinary 
Engineering Education and Practice category in 2026.
