Luca de Alfaro obtained his Ph.D. from Stanford University in 1998, with a thesis on logics for the specification of probabilistic systems. 
After a 3-year postdoc in the group of Professor Henzinger at UC Berkeley, he joined UCSC in 2001. 
His research interests have included game theory, reputation systems, crowdsourcing, networking, and more recently, 
data science and computational applications to ecology. 
While at UCSC, he took leaves of absence to work at Google, where he developed reputation systems for Maps edits. 
He co-founded two startups: SSB Progetti in Italy, later acquired by Deloitte, and Camio in the US. 
His work received four test-of-time awards for contributions to game theory and software engineering. 
He was elected Foreign Member of the Turin Academy of Sciences in 2024, and a Fellow of the ACM in 2025.
