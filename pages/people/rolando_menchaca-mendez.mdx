Rolando Menchaca-Méndez is a Professor and founder of the Network and Data Science Laboratory at the Center for Computing Research of the National Polytechnic Institute (CIC-IPN), Mexico. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from the University of California, Santa Cruz, in 2009. He is the recipient of the 1999 ANIEI Best Mexican Master's Thesis Award, co-recipient of the IEEE MASS 2008 Best Paper Award, and coauthor of the Spotlight Paper for the August 2012 issue of IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing.
Dr. Menchaca-Méndez has published over 150 research papers in journals, conferences, and books. His current research interests include combinatorial optimization, reinforcement learning, graph neural networks, and network protocols for IoT and Smart Cities. A member of the Mexican National System of Researchers and former CIO of the Mexican Ministry of Public Administration, he takes great pride in his growing network of alumni, which includes 13 Ph.D. and 57 M.S. graduates.
