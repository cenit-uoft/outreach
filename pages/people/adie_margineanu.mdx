Adie Margineanu is the Administrative Assistant for CENIT at the University of Toronto. 
She brings more than 14 years of experience in user experience research, service design, project coordination, and 
digital transformation, including three years as User Experience Lead at U of T Scarborough. 
Her background in collaborating across diverse stakeholder groups and improving organizational processes supports 
CENIT's mission and team members.
