Dr. Ersin Uzun is the Katherine Johnson Executive Director of the ESL Global Cybersecurity Institute and a Professor in the Golisano College of Computing and Information Sciences at the Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT). Before joining RIT, he was the Vice President and Director of Computer Science and Systems Sciences Labs at Palo Alto Research Center. His research interests include network/system security, privacy enhancing technologies and human factors in security privacy. 
Professor Uzun has M.S. and PhD degrees in computer science from University of California, Irvine and inductee of its Engineering and Computer Science Hall of Fame. He has over 200 global patents and research publications to date and been a PI/Co-PI on many projects sponsored by government agencies (NSF, DARPA, DoD, DoE, OTF, NSA), non-profits (e.g., Google Foundation, Omidyar Networks, ESL Foundation) and corporations (e.g., Lenovo, IBM, Samsung, Google, Sandvik, NBC, Daikin, Hitachi, P&G, Boeing) .
