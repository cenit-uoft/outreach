Folake is a Senior Research Analyst at CENIT, University of Toronto. 
She leads ecosystem mapping and social network analysis of Canada’s internet service innovation ecosystem, examining relationships 
across academic, government, and industry stakeholders to identify structural gaps, key actors and connections, 
and opportunities to strengthen Canada’s innovation ecosystem. Her work combines quantitative network analysis with mixed-methods and 
equity-focused research approaches to generate evidence and strategic insights for policy and institutional decision-making.


Folake holds a PhD in Chemical Engineering from the University of Toronto, 
with a focus on food engineering, an MSc in Chemical Process Engineering from University College London, 
and a BSc in Chemical Engineering from Obafemi Awolowo University. Her broader research interests include innovation ecosystems, 
applied research and technology development, evidence-informed strategy, product and process innovation, and the translation of 
research into scalable solutions with real-world impact.
