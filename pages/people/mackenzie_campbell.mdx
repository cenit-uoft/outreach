MacKenzie is a double graduate of the University of Toronto with a Bachelor of Applied Science (BASc) in Engineering Science majoring 
in Biomedical Systems and a Master of Applied Science (MASc) in Chemical Engineering with a Specialization in Engineering Education. 
Her interests are in the intersection of equity, policy, and systems. As a Research Analyst with CENIT, she focused on internet 
ecosystem mapping, digital divide, and case studies of internet infrastructure development particularly in Indigenous communities.
