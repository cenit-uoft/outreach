Dr. Abdulazaz Albalawi is the Dean of the University College of Duba and an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Tabuk, Saudi Arabia. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from the University of California, Santa Cruz, USA, in 2021. Prior to joining the University of Tabuk, he gained research and industry experience at Futurewei, Arista Networks, and Nokia. His research interests include transport protocols, information-centric networking, and networking for emerging and distributed applications.
