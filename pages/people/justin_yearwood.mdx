Justin Yearwood is a recent graduate of the University of Toronto and a Research Analyst with the CENIT Research Group, 
where he conducts research under the supervision of Professor Philip Asare. He earned his Bachelor of Applied Science (BASc) 
degree in Computer Engineering from the University of Toronto, with a Minor in Engineering Business. His current research 
focuses on bringing greater awareness to the urban digital divide by examining fine-grained differences in internet availability, 
service offerings, and pricing across communities. His broader research interests include digital infrastructure and equity, 
particularly how data and technology can be used to better understand disparities in access to essential digital services.
