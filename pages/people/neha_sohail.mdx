Neha is a Computer Science graduate of the University of Toronto. She has worked as a Software Engineer at Vidyard and Xero, 
where she gained experience developing full-stack applications and distributed workflow orchestration systems. 
She has also worked as a Research Analyst under the supervision of Professor Philip Asare in University of Toronto’s Institute 
for Studies in Transdisciplinary Engineering Education and Practice (ISTEP), where she developed a rubric assessment platform 
designed to support a range of assessment workflows, with academic assessment as one of its core use cases. 
As part of the UofT CENIT group, she developed a dashboard to track Internet Service Provider (ISP) service offerings in various 
locations in the Greater Toronto Area to understand urban segregation of internet availability and performance, with the broader 
aim of advancing accessible and equitable technology. Neha holds an AWS certification and has a strong interest in microservices, 
distributed systems, and cloud infrastructure.
