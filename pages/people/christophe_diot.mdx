Christophe Diot received a Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from INP Grenoble in 1991. Throughout his 35 years career, 
Diot pioneered diffserv, single source multicast, ad-hoc communication, peer-to-peer online games, unsupervised anomaly 
detection and most importantly Internet measurements. After five years in public research at INRIA, Diot spent his career in industry, 
building R&D labs at Sprint (Bay area), INTEL Research (Cambridge, UK), and Technicolor (Paris and Palo Alto). He was the Chief Scientist 
at Technicolor between 2009 and 2015. He helped launch Safran Analytics as their CTO before joining GOOGLE in June 2018 as a Principal 
Engineer. Diot designed telemetry solutions for improving Google’s network availability. At Google, he was also the corporate sponsor 
for M-LAB. Diot has around 30 patents and more than 200 publications in major conferences and journals, with an H index of 84. 
He is an ACM fellow.
