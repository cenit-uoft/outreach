Renato M. de Moraes received the Bachelor degree in Electrical Engineering from Federal University of Pernambuco, Brazil in 1996, 
and the Master degree from State University of Campinas (UNICAMP), Sao Paulo, Brazil in 1998. He obtained his Ph.D. degree in 
EE from University of California, Santa Cruz, USA in 2005. 
Currently, Renato is Associate Professor at Centro de Informatica (CIn), Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE), Brazil. 
Renato has served as TPC member for IEEE WCNC 2026, 2025, 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 and 2020, 
IEEE ISWCS 2018, IEEE MASS 2016, 2017 and 2018, IWCMC 2007 and 2008, IEEE PIMRC 2012, IEEE ICCVE 2013, 2014 and 2015, 
IEEE Globecom 2013, 2014 and 2015, ICT 2014, IEEE WCNC 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016 and 2017, 
publication chair for IEEE SPAWC 2011 and Technical Program Committee Chair for 
IWCMC 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016 and 2017, and SBrT 2012. He has published more than 50 publications. 
His areas of interest include ad hoc networks, information theory and communication networks. 
He is an IEEE Senior Member.
