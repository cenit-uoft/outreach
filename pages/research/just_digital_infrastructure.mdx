# JEDI: Just and Equitable Digital Infrastructures

# ![img](/research/jedi.png)

A major goal in CENIT is to develop knowledge necessary for creating just and equitable digital infrastructures (JEDI). 
To achieve this goal, the research program will approach the work in a way that is socio-economically aware and community-engaged. 
The Figure visually represents the method of approach advocated for this research thrust. This research thrust will focus on developing 
socio-technical models of intelligent information infrastructures and use that to inform the development of novel network 
architectures and protocols that enable just and equitable digital infrastructures. The model would capture end-user needs and factors 
affecting network development, network deployments, and network service availability. Justice and equity would be captured in terms of 
the extent to which an instantiation of network deployment and service availability meets the needs of users. This model would help in 
explaining the current imbalance between user needs and available service and the current digital divide. 
It would also provide a framework for development and preliminary assessment of requirements for future networks that address 
these current challenges.
