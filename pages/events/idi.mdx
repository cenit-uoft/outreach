####  Background

CENIT's work under the Canada Excellence Research Chair in Intelligent Digital Infrastructures (CERC-IDI) asks a simple question: 
what could Internet protocols look like if we designed them for today's computing power instead of the constraints of the 1970s 
and 80s? The workshop gave our collaborators and advisory board a snapshot of our progress and let us ask for 
their honest feedback. We asked for it on the technology, on commercialization, on the ease of use, and on real-world impacts.
