## CENIT Community Engagements

The <a href="/research/just_digital_infrastructure" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">JEDI</a>
 thrust of CENIT  is seeking community-partners to accomplish three goals: 
(1) understand the challenges and limitations faced by different communities of users in terms of accessing 
the internet and other digital networks, 
(2) understand the needs and goals of different communities of users in terms of digital accessibility, 
and once user needs are well-understood (3) test potential technological solutions and service delivery models 
that address these gaps and needs and solicit community feedback to ensure appropriate fit of these technologies. 
The followings are some of the ways that we’ve built partnerships so far: 

- June 2026-present: <a href="/people/lindsay_montgomery" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">Prof. Lindsay M. Montgomery</a>
 developed a collaboration with Picuris Pueblo and Terentia, 
a cloud-based software company in Toronto that provides digital asset management to Tribal Nations and Indigenous communities. 
The ongoing community-engaged project centers on the construction of web-based collections managements system and digital workflow 
processes grounded in OCAP principles. 

- January 2026-present: <a href="/people/lindsay_montgomery" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">Prof. Lindsay M. Montgomery</a>
developed a collaboration with Dr. Silvia Sarapura-Escobar 
(University of Guelph) as part of a larger project entitled “Pathways to Indigenous Food Sovereignty: Integrating Relational Systems 
Thinking, Food Environments, and Community Learning Hubs”. The project brings together Indigenous community members from across Canada, 
the United States, and Latin America to develop community governed databases that ethically archive traditional ecological 
knowledge and foodways. A key component of the collaboration involves enhancing community access to these platforms and building 
digital literacy among partners.
