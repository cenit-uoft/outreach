## Talks by CENIT Members

### Talks on Just and Equitable Digital Infrastructures (JEDI)

- <a href="/people/philip_asare" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">Philip Asare</a>
 “Value of Engineering” EngTalks, Ontario Society of Professional Engineers (OSPE) September 16, 2026: https://site.pheedloop.com/event/engtalks/home
 [lead facilitator with Lisa Romkey, Norris Ma, Dimpho Radebe as co-facilitators]. 
Philip Asare and his co-facilitators will be facilitating discussion with members of the engineering community and actors that engage 
with the community on how to steer the profession to deliver more societal value. 
This orientation of the community is important to delivering some of the projects related to 
<a href="/research/just_digital_infrastructure" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">JEDI</a>.

- <a href="/people/philip_asare" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">Philip Asare</a>,
 “Black Engineers Shaping Canada's Future: Canada’s Global Role in STEM and International Trade” Black Engineers of Canada National 
 Conference: Build & Bridge: https://www.blackengineers.ca/events/building-bridges-creating-inclusive-futures August 14, 2026 
 [invited keynote speaker] Philip Asare will be delivering a keynote on ways to think about trade and value delivery to the 
 Black engineering community in Canada and ways to think about opportunities for engineering and technology to help society. 
 Philip will leverage the innovations ecosystems framework that is a key part of the <a href="/research/just_digital_infrastructure" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">JEDI</a>.

- <a href="/people/philip_asare" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">Philip Asare</a>
 “Creating Inclusive Workplaces” Black Engineers of Canada National Conference: Build & Bridge: 
 https://www.blackengineers.ca/events/building-bridges-creating-inclusive-futures August 14, 2026  
 [invited panelist] Inclusive perspectives within organizations and across actors is one of the innovation ecosystem 
 levers that are important to delivering on the <a href="/research/just_digital_infrastructure" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">JEDI</a>.
  It is important for those with the lived experience 
 in equity-deserving groups to be represented in decision-making about ecosystem directions. Philip Asare will be part of a panel 
 discussing the importance of this approach and steps to move the broader engineering ecosystem in this direction.

- <a href="/people/philip_asare" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">Philip Asare</a>,
 “Transcending Boundaries: Working at the Intersection of People and Technology” SkuleTM Lunch & Learn, 
 University of Toronto: https://alumni.engineering.utoronto.ca/event/ll-philipasare/ Feb 11, 2026 [invited speaker] 
 [recording embargoed---to be added when made open] This talk highlighted the innovation ecosystems framework that underlies 
 the work on <a href="/research/just_digital_infrastructure" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">JEDI</a>
  and also discussed some of the progress on the <a href="/research/just_digital_infrastructure" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">JEDI</a>
   as part of the examples.

- <a href="/people/lindsay_montgomery" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">Lindsay M. Montgomery</a>
 (organizer), <a href="/people/philip_asare" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">Philip Asare</a>
  (facilitator). “Building Indigenous Futures through Archaeology & Public History” workshop. 
 Saskatoon, SK (April 29-May 1). This workshop brought together over 20 Indigenous scholars and students working across 
 Canada to share knowledge and experiences around the challenges of cultural heritage management. Philip Asare facilitated 
 a workshop on how to think about the digital technology needs to support the geographically-distributed network which is essential 
 to understanding community needs as part of <a href="/research/just_digital_infrastructure" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">JEDI</a>. 
 This event was developed as part of a larger 
 SSHRC Connections Grant funded project oriented around developing a Canadian Indigenous Archaeology network.

- <a href="/people/lindsay_montgomery" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-bold text-[#35538E] hover:underline">Lindsay M. Montgomery</a>
 (presenter), “Indigenous Data Sovereignty: A Critical Consideration of UNDRIP’s Implications for Canadian Archaeology,” 
 Computer Applications in Archaeology Conference, April 8-12, 2024, Auckland, NZ. This talk engaged with how to integrate Indigenous 
 Data Sovereignty, including Ownership, Control, Access, and Possession (OCAP) and Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable (FAIR) 
 principles, into the development of web-based databases and archival systems.
