####  Background

Just and Equitable Digital Infrastructures (JEDI) is a major research thrust of CENIT. Alongside our work on reimagining 
how the Internet operates, JEDI asks: who does digital infrastructure serve, and how can we build it to better serve all
segments of society? The workshop brought together people from across the digital ecosystem to reflect on current JEDI 
research, map relationships within the ecosystem, and explore possibilities for change.
